Exclusive Opportunity

THE SIXTY TWO™ Society is located at the premier location in Vail Village, at the base of Vail Mountain just steps from Gondola One.

THE SIXTY TWO™ Society is designed for those who appreciate a more refined, personal way to experience Vail. What sets it apart is its independence, limited membership, and commitment to a seamless experience.

Exclusive Ski Access & Ski Valet Services in Partnership with Gorsuch

Fully embrace the day's adventures with ease. Members' equipment will be staged and ready to go at THE SIXTY TWO™ Society. The Gorsuch team will manage all ski-membership needs including ski storage, valet, demos, boot warming, rentals, and more.

Personalized Concierge Services

Enjoy your Society concierge services, available in person, through THE SIXTY TWO™ Society, or via a dedicated Society-only number. Effortlessly schedule personal transportation, secure priority dining reservations, and arrange a variety of activities tailored to your preferences.

Frequently Asked

Your Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about membership, access, timing, and the vision behind The Society.

What is THE SIXTY TWO™ Society Vail?
THE SIXTY TWO™ Society is a privately owned, membership-based social, dining, and hospitality club located at the base of Gondola One in Vail Village.
Who is THE SIXTY TWO™ Society designed for?
Designed for individuals and families with a strong connection to Vail, including residents, second homeowners, and frequent visitors.
Who is at the helm of THE SIXTY TWO™ Society?
Founded and operated by Brad Kaemmer, a Vail native and hospitality professional.
What is the vision for arrival?
A seamless slope side entry connecting ski access, dining, and social spaces, including a convivial outdoor fire pit.
What amenities and services are available to Members?
Includes ski storage / ski valet and year-round dining, concierge services, transportation, social events, and reciprocal privileges.
What ski services are included with membership?
Ski storage, ski valet, boot warming, rentals, and retail access through Gorsuch.
How do transportation, reservations, and events work?
Managed through concierge team and Member mobile app.
Is THE SIXTY TWO™ Restaurant open to the public?
Accessible to the public, but Members receive priority across reservations and seating.
What membership designations are available?
Founder Society Membership (Non-Refundable), Founder Circle Society Membership (Refundable).
How many memberships will be offered?
A total of 62 memberships are offered.
Is there a waiting list?
Not currently. A waiting list may be established once capacity is reached.
Who is permitted to access THE SIXTY TWO™ Society Vail?
Members, accompanied guests, and adult children within defined guidelines.
Are there annual dues?
Yes. Annual dues apply to most membership types.
Is this an equity club or investment?
No. Membership is a revocable license and does not include ownership.
Can membership be included in a real estate transaction?
Yes. Membership may be incorporated into a real estate purchase or sale.
Is there a membership option for real estate professionals or builders?
Yes, subject to approval.
Can memberships be transferred or inherited?
Yes, in specific scenarios and subject to approval.
What happens if I resign my membership?
Members may resign annually; some cases allow prorated refunds.
How do I apply for membership?
Through an application process by contacting the Membership team.
When is the projected opening date?
Planned to open in Summer 2026.
Why now?
Opportunity to secure membership before capacity is reached.
Will Members have access during peak season?
Yes, access is protected by limited membership.
Is membership year-round or seasonal?
Year-round with social experiences and dining.
How does THE SIXTY TWO™ Society differ from other Vail clubs?
Locally owned, boutique hospitality experience with limited membership.
